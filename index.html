      How to play

      The loop. Cross each region's map, fight for loot, salvage it into essences, forge better gear, and beat the boss at the end of the map to open the next region.

      The map. You only see what's on the nodes right next to you. Fights, elites (harder, but they drop an essence), chests, shrines, peddlers and mystery nodes. The row before the boss is always a camp. Losing sends you back to a fresh map; cleared regions can be explored again.

      Health. HP carries over between fights. Heal by eating, resting at a campfire (one charge per three wins), camps and shrines. Healing traits on gear mend you each turn.

      Defeat. You wake at the region's edge with 30% HP, and a scavenger takes one unequipped item.

      Gear. Seven slots. An off-hand weapon adds a weaker second strike; a shield can block hits.

      Trait effects. Fire burns, Ice can freeze, Poison stacks, Holy heals you, Shadow drains life, Sharp raises crits, Heavy pierces armor, Swift gives extra strikes, Steam makes enemies miss.

      Knowledge. You learn a weakness by hitting it, a resistance by being resisted, a special move by surviving it, stats by winning and drops by finding them. Study reveals one fact but costs your turn.

      Enemy moves. Special moves are announced a turn ahead, and the creature glows red. Guard takes 60% less damage.

      Mixing. Only sensible combinations work; the Recipes tab has the table. Salvage turns any item into essences, which mix with everything.

      Infusions. Pure essences can be infused into yourself in the Gear tab. The trait stays with you whatever you wield.

      Previews. Known recipes show their full result before you forge. New combinations show the form, mishap risk and any reactions you've discovered. From Smith Lv 3 the forge also appraises the traits to expect.
