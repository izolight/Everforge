# Everforge

A mobile-first crafting and battling game. Fight through four regions, salvage loot into essences, and forge better gear. It runs entirely in the browser, with no build step and no dependencies. Progress is stored in `localStorage`; the Codex tab can export and import a save code.

## Run it

Open `index.html` in a browser, or serve the folder with any static server (`python3 -m http.server`). It loads three fonts from Google Fonts and falls back to system fonts offline.

## Conventions

- The scripts are plain (non-module) scripts that share one global scope, so they work from `file://` without a server. **Load order matters**; it is the order of the `<script>` tags in `index.html`. Each file may use anything defined by files above it at load time, and anything at all inside functions at call time.
- All game state lives in the single global `S` (see `freshState()` in `state.js`). After changing it, call `save()` and `render()`.
- Weapon-form and shield abilities live in the `ABILITIES` table (`data.js`); `ability(key)` in `battle.js` runs them, cooldowns are per battle in `B.cd`, and `effects()` reads the same table for item text.
- Damage model (`data.js`, used by `strike`, `foeHit` in `battle.js` and `pstats()`): a blow does `base²/(base+armor)` where `base` is half the strike power (`Attack + Magic x MAG_W`) and `armor` is the enemy's Defense (less Tempered shred, less what Heavy/Lunge/Channel ignore), so armor takes a share of a hit but never all of it. An enemy hit is cut by `redOf(def)=def/(def+DEF_K)`, after your element resist, then by Stone's flat `dr`, but resist, Defense and Stone together never cut it below `HIT_MIN` of its raw damage; Heat 5 enemies bite through `HEAT_T.bite` of your Defense first. A parry on a telegraphed special lands `PARRY_CH` of the time. Change these constants, not the formulas, and re-run `tools/sim.js` (`effects()`, the Heat text and the rules paragraph in the Codex fill their numbers in from the same constants).
- Heat (`HEAT`, `HEAT_T` in `data.js`) is stored per zone in `S.heat[zone]={max,sel}`; a map remembers the heat it was generated with (`map.heat`) and `heatNow()` reads it. Every Heat level adds `HEAT_T.hp` HP and `HEAT_T.atk` Attack to all enemies (so Heat 5 is +HEAT_T.hp x5 HP and +HEAT_T.atk x5 Attack), on top of the per-level rules in `HEAT`. The daily lives in `S.daily` (not `S.maps`); while `S.run==='daily'`, `curMap()` returns its map and `S.zone` is the daily's zone, restored from `S.daily.back` by `finishDaily()`. Daily maps come from `genMap({seed,...})`, which uses the seeded `rng` instead of `Math.random`.
- Bosses can carry a data-driven `phase` (see `archon` in `data.js`): below `at` of max HP they spend a turn changing form, and `foeOf(B)` returns the current form's fields (element, weak, resist, special, status). Use `foeOf(B)` rather than `ENEMIES[B.id]` for anything that depends on the form.
- Adding a zone: append to `ZONES` and give it enemies, a boss and a `terrain()` look in `art.js`. Unlocking, the mimic scaling (`MIMIC_HP`/`MIMIC_ATK`), bounties and the daily pick it up; `migrate()` opens zones for saves that already beat the boss before them; a daily boss kill unlocks the next zone too (same rule, so a reload never changes what is open).
- Maps are tile grids (`GRID` 9x13 in `data.js`): `m.g` terrain rows (`.` floor, `,` brush, `#` rock/tree, `~` water; the last two block), `m.pois` (`{t,x,y,r,done,...}`; `r` is depth 0-5 by walking distance from the start, 6 = the lair; a seeded daily map adds the same pre-roll fields per POI as it did per node), `m.log` (map log lines, last 3 shown under the map), `m.sn` seen flags, `m.mem` last-seen POI types, `m.pos`/`m.prev`. `step(x,y)` moves (4-way), drains the torch, reveals (`reveal`, Chebyshev light radius `TORCH.rad`, `TORCH.dim` when the torch is out) and calls `enter(poi)`, the single resolver for battles and events. At torch 0 each step rolls an ambush (`TORCH.amb`, seeded per step on the daily). `torchUp(n)` refills (camp full, shrine prayer, campfire rest); Heat 5 drains `TORCH.h5` per step. `fallBack(m)` is the boss flee. **Roaming enemies:** every regular enemy has an `mv` in `ENEMIES` (`still`, `wander` or `chase`; `ROAM` in `data.js` holds the tunables); genMap picks each fight/elite POI's enemy up front (`p.e`) and copies the movement type to `p.mv` (elites always `chase`, slowly; the gate guard and boss never move; none start within 3 tiles of the start). After each step that starts no encounter, and never on the first step, `foesAct(m)` lets every live mover act once in `m.pois` order: wanderers step to a random free neighbour (or stay, 40%); a chaser notices you within 4 path steps (6 with the torch out, +1 at Heat 3+; elites within 2), then follows a BFS path until you are over 8 steps away; elites act every other step. Enemies never enter rock, water, the start, gate or lair tiles or any non-enemy POI, and never share a tile; an enemy that steps onto you starts the fight through `enter(p,"it",from)`. The depth scaling stays `p.r` from generation. State lives on the POI (`x`,`y`,`aw` aware, `dz` daze steps, `pa` elite parity, `ms` last step it moved), so saves need no version bump; a daily seeds its wander rolls per step (`m.seed`+`m.steps`) and survives a reload. Fleeing a map enemy (`slip(B)`, `B.pi`/`B.by`/`B.ef` say which, who moved and where it came from) leaves it alive and dazed for `ROAM.daze` steps and puts you back on the tile you came from (or leaves you in place if it walked into you). Maps already in a save from before this keep their `mv:"still"` enemies; new expeditions roam. Only lit tiles show live enemies; a remembered tile shows a faded last-seen marker. Map tiles use the classes `.tl`/`.tls` (not `.tile`/`.grid`, which are taken).
- **Affixes (rarity tiers, `affix.js`; tables in `data.js`).** A map enemy (fight, elite, ambush; never a boss or the mimic) is **normal** (no affix), **magic** (1 affix) or **rare** (2 affixes, 3 from Heat `RAR_CH.k3` = 4). Rolls: an elite tile is magic or rare, with the rare share `RAR_CH.rare` = 15% + 5% per Heat + 3% per depth row `r` (capped at 60%); an ordinary fight turns magic from Heat 2 (10%, +5% per further Heat); dark ambushes roll like fights. `rollFoe(R,{elite,heat,row,mv})` returns `{rar,afx}` and `rollAfx` picks the affixes; both take the rng as `R`, so the daily pre-rolls them per tile from its own seeded rng (`hash(seed:r:x,y)`, so the other pre-rolls are untouched) and a walked daily meets the same foes whatever `Math.random` does. POIs store `p.rar` and `p.afx` (ids), rolled once so fleeing and returning meets the same foe; mystery ambushes and unrolled old maps roll when entered. `startBattle(id,{rar,afx})` (or `elite:true` to roll an elite) applies them with `applyAfx(B,E,rar,afx)`: **`RARITIES`** gives HP and Attack multipliers (magic x1.35 HP, x1.15 Attack; rare x1.55, x1.2; `def` adds Defense), each affix's `m` adds its own (elemental +1 Defense, Regenerating x0.9 HP, Elusive and Splitting x0.85 HP, Swift-footed x1.05 Attack), and a battle stores `B.rar`, `B.afx`, `B.title`. Name: the first affix's prefix plus the species ("Vampiric Frost Wolf"); a rare adds its second affix's title ("Umbral Frost Wolf the Bloodthirsty"); the HUD clamps the name to two lines. Colours: magic blue and rare gold, the Greater and Pure grade colours. Two affixes never combine when they share a group (`g`, space-separated: one elemental; one of Warded/Regenerating/Elusive; one of Vampiric/Regenerating/Warded; one of Splitting/Volatile) or the same essence; **Swift-footed** is only rolled for species that move. `B.el` and the family resist come from the elemental affix, if any (`elTr(B)`), otherwise from the species; the old elite status chance (`ELITE_INF`) follows that affix.
- **Affix table** (`AFX`, numbers in `AFX_T`; each hook is one helper in `affix.js` called from the combat flow, and every effect has a log line and a tip on its HUD chip): the eight **elemental** affixes keep the old elite behaviour (Burning/Fire, Frostbound/Ice, Venomous/Poison, Hallowed/Holy, Umbral/Shadow, Ironclad/Iron, Fleet/Swift, Arcane/Arcane: fights as that family, resists it, loses any weakness to it, +1 Defense, 25% status on hit for Fire, Ice, Poison and Shadow; drops that essence). The behaviour affixes, with the essence each drops:

  | Affix (title) | Effect | Essence |
  |---|---|---|
  | Vampiric (Bloodthirsty) | heals 40% of the damage it deals you (`leech`, in `enemyTurn`) | Nourishing |
  | Thorned (Barbed) | reflects 15% of a blow that lands, at most 6% of your max HP per round, never lethal (`thornBack`, in `strike`) | Sharp |
  | Regenerating (Undying) | heals 3% of max HP at the start of its turn (`afxTick`); burn and poison offset it | Wood |
  | Frenzied (Savage) | below 40% HP its Attack x1.4, once, with a log line (`frenzy`, checked after every hit and tick) | Heavy |

  Rewards: every affix drops its essence when the foe falls, graded by `rollGrade(zone,heat,x,up)` with `up` = `RARITIES[rar].gs` grade steps better (a fraction is the chance of one step), never above the best grade the zone's `GRADE_ODDS` can roll. Embers: `EMB.rar` (magic 5, rare 8; the old elite paid 5). **`gs` is 0.5 (magic) and 1 (rare), not a whole step and two:** with whole steps essence income jumped far past the 20-25% budget (`tools/sim.js --econ-only` at Heat 3, `ess+sv`: hollow +41%, spire +37%); with these values the total (`ess+sv`) stays within about +25% at Heat 0 and 3 and embers within 6%. The direct `ess` column rises more (affix essences are its main source). Raise `gs` to 1 and 2 for the spec'd steps and re-run the sim. The Codex's Affixes list reads `S.afx[id]={s,b}` (fights met in, enemies beaten; `noteAfx`), so an entry opens when you first meet the affix. Save v13: map POIs' old `up`/`af` and an open battle's `elite` migrate to a magic enemy with that elemental affix, and `S.afx` is new. The Heat text and the Codex's affix rows fill their numbers from the same constants.
- UI building blocks (compact, icon-first; use them for new views): **Tooltips** (`tip.js`): any element with `data-tip="Title\nline\nline"` gets the single global popover. Build the attribute with `tt(title,...lines)` (escapes it; text only). A non-interactive element (chip, stat) toggles it on tap; a button, link or summary keeps its tap action and shows it on long-press (the press that opens it does not also act), or on hover/focus on desktop. `helpBtn(title,...lines)` makes a small "?" button that toggles on tap (use it in section headers, also inside a `<summary>`, where it does not toggle the details). It is dismissed by an outside tap, scroll or Escape, flips and clamps to the viewport, sets `aria-describedby` on its anchor, and a MutationObserver gives tip-only elements `tabindex`/`role="note"`/`aria-label`, so no wiring is needed after `render()`. Don't use `title=`: it is invisible on touch. **Icons** (`art.js`): `ic(name,size)` draws a 24px line icon from `NODE_G` (size classes `xs` 11px, `s` 13px, default 16px, `l` 22px); `famIc(family)` is the icon of a trait family (`f_fire`, `f_ice`...), `stIc(stat)` of a stat (`atk`, `mag`, `def`, `spd`, `heal`), `gIc(form)` a flat item glyph, `skIcon(skill)` a skill's. Add an icon as one line in `NODE_G` (`filter` and `sort` are there, unused). **Chips**: `chips(traits)` is icon + number (pass `true` for the name too, as the item sheet does), `statGrid(stats,cmp,full)` icon + number per stat, `cmpBlock(item,other,label)` a one-row comparison; put the words in `tt(...)`. **Satchel tiles**: `.sq` squares in a 5-column `.sqg` (see `satchel()`): item art, count badge `.sq-n`, worn tick `.sq-w`, new dot `.sq-d`, grade frame from `--gc`, rarity stripe from `--rc` (`rcol(it)` sets both), `.no` dims; `itemTip(it,...extra)` is the tooltip text. `tabBtn(attr,key,label,on)` makes an icon filter chip (`attr` is kept for U2's Recipes tabs: `tabBtn("rtab",...)`) (the active one shows its label); Recipes and the Satchel both use it.
- U2 additions to the building blocks: `ci(color,icons,value,...tip)` (render.js) is a one-line icon chip; `.ibt` is a square icon button (a `.cdb` badge counts charges or cooldowns; give a disabled-looking one `aria-disabled="true" data-tap` (the arena handler ignores it) so a tap still shows its tip); `ic()` gained `weak`, `heat`, `daily`, `close`, `list`, `save`. **Battle layout:** the expedition view is a two-row status strip (`mapHead()`: HP, ATK/DEF, torch, daily/Heat badges, camp rest `#rest`, abandon `#dailyAbandon`, and one "?" holding the map rules and every modifier) over the map. The Heat picker opens from `#heatBtn` (`heatOpen`; its `[data-heat]` chips only exist while open). `fitMap()` narrows `.map` (CSS var `--mw`, never under 36px tiles) so the whole grid sits above the tab bar, and `.tl.here` scrolls into view with a margin of `--nav` (the tab bar height). The legend is the collapsed `#legend` (`legendOpen`). In a fight, `youHud()`/`foeStage()` show icon chips (statuses, weak/resist, badges; details in tips), `.intent` is one row (special name, damage `~n`, Parry %), `.acts` is a 4-column grid (Attack, abilities, then Guard/Parry/Study/Flee; cooldown in `.cdb`; descriptions in tips, long-press on touch), consumables and loot are `.sq` squares, and the log shows two lines with `#logd` to expand. **Recipes/Codex:** one `.rr` row per recipe (art, ingredient icons crossed out when missing, `.ibt.load`); the mix table, trait reactions (`.rx1`, `?` when unknown) and secrets (`.sec` squares) are icon grids with tips; the Codex is a `.cxt` portrait grid per region, and the save box and "How to play" are collapsed `details`.
- **Unlocks and intros (`UNLOCKS` in `data.js`, logic in `unlocks.js`).** Systems open as you play. `S.unl[id]` = the order a system opened in; `S.seen["i:"+id]` = its intro was read; `S.seen["a:"+form]` = that weapon form's ability was introduced. Each `UNLOCKS` entry has `id`, `name`, `ic` (an icon in `NODE_G`), `when(s)` (a pure test over a state: Smith level via `lvOf(s.xp)`, `s.wins`, `exps(s)` (finished expeditions: boss kills plus defeats), a boss kill, `s.quest`, a stat, `has_(s,"other")`; omit it for a hook that calls `unlock(id)` itself, like Parry and Hunters), `used(s)` (migration only: proof an old save already used it), `txt` (the teaser: how to unlock it), `w` (the toast hint), `q` (quiet: no toast or sound) and `i` (the intro, below). `checkUnl()` runs at the start of every `render()` and in `newMap()` (so a defeat or boss kill opens POI types on the very next map); a new unlock plays `sfx("unlock")` and a toast that waits behind any toast the same action raised (`toast(m,1)`). **Gating a thing:** test `isUnl(id)` where it is drawn and draw `lockRow(id)` (a lock, the name, how to open it, same words in its tip) where a teaser builds anticipation (tabs' sections, the action grid via `lockAct(id)`); hide it where a teaser would be noise (map POI types, the ember counter, Heat, the Codex's stats and affixes). `genMap` places a POI type only if `isUnl(type)` (the `ok(t)` helper; a seeded daily map ignores it, and the rolls are made either way so the rest of the layout does not shift); `foesAct` does nothing before `roam`.   **Intros:** `i:{t,l:[1-3 short second-person lines],tab,sel,ctx}` per entry. Show them one at a time: `pendingIntros()` is the unlocked unread ones in opening order (plus a card for each weapon ability not yet introduced), `curIntro(inFight)` the one on show. A card is `introIn(c)` (icon, title, lines, a **Got it** button `#introOk` that calls `dismissIntro(id)`), never modal, never takes focus, and never stacks. `ctx:"battle"` cards (Study, Parry, abilities) show only in a fight, above the action grid, and wait out of fights; the others show on their `tab` (floating over the map on the Battle tab so the map never shifts; at the top of the other tabs, with a pulsing dot on that tab's nav button until you open it). When a card appears, `nudge(sel)` pulses what `sel` points at for 2.6s (a still outline under reduced motion). The Codex **Guide** (`renderGuide`) lists every unlocked intro to re-read, with a Show me button, and every locked system as a teaser. **Adding a system (G2):** add an entry (order = Guide order), gate its UI with `isUnl`, give it an intro and a `TUTORIAL` step if it deserves one, and add its proof to `used` so old saves keep it. G2's crafting techniques (fusions, multi-essence tempering, grade upgrades, reforge and refine, legendary attempts) are not gated yet; the upgrade panel's `upgrade` entry is the one hook that exists, and a research unlock can replace its `when`. Keep intro text to what is unlocked: never mention a system that is still locked.
- Save v14: `S.unl` is new. `grandfather(s)` (state.js, last step of `migrate()` for a v13 save) opens every system whose `when` holds or whose `used` finds proof, and marks those intros read; anyone with the first boss down, 10 wins, 3 recipes or a daily is a veteran and gets everything (the old game showed everything from the start). `S.quest` of 7 or more becomes the new chain's end, so veterans skip the new goal steps.
- When the save shape changes, bump `KEY`/`v`, add a migration step in `migrate()`, and extend `sane()` if new required fields are added.
- Secrets (`SECRETS`, optional `bonus:{trait,v}` merged by `combine()`) start hidden except the first three. `S.hints` lists secret indexes; each repeat of an index reveals one more ingredient (`hintLv`, `giveHint(x)` in `goals.js`). Hint scrolls come from chests, shrine offerings, riddles, travellers, boss first kills, bounties and the daily.
- Map events live in `ENC[type]={make(rv,hl),pick(choice,ev)}`; `rv` is four rolls pre-rolled per POI (`poi.rv`) in the seeded daily map, plus four more (`rv[4..7]`, from `poi.gr`) that roll the grade of whatever the event gives. Satchel-dependent choices vary per player. Every event must finish in `endEv(...)` (no choices) or a leave.
- Embers (`S.embers`, tunables in `EMB` in `data.js`) come from fights (`fightEmbers`), salvage and selling. The peddler is a shop (`merchant(sh)`; the stock is pre-rolled per POI as `poi.sh` on the seeded daily). Quality grades: essences and basic materials (ores, hides, herbs; not relics or gear) come in 5 grades, Faint/Lesser/Plain/Greater/Pure (`GRADES`, `GRADE_M` in `data.js`). A graded item has the id `<base>@<g>` (`ess_Fire@4`, `golem_core@2`); **Plain keeps the bare id**, so SECRETS, recipes and old saves are untouched (secrets only match Plain; a graded stand-in logs why). Use `gradeOf`/`baseIdOf`/`gradedId` (pure, `data.js`), `gradedItem(base,g)`/`essG(trait,g)` (create the item on demand) and `isEss`/`essTrait`; never parse `ess_` ids by hand. Drops roll a grade with `rollGrade(zone,heat,x)` (`GRADE_ODDS`, Heat shifts it; `gdrop`/`gess` for a zone, `dropNow`/`essNow` for the current one); `x` is a 0-1 roll so seeded daily outcomes pre-roll it (`poi.gr`, the fraction left of a shop roll). Upgrading (Forge tab, `upgrade(id)`, `UP` and `GRADE_LV`): 3 of a grade + embers make 1 of the next, with an Alchemy-skill success chance (a miss loses 1 of the 3) and an Alchemy level needed per grade. Infusion counts essences by `INF_W` (Greater = 3 plain, Pure = 9, Lesser = 1/3, Faint = 1/9) and `infPay(t)` picks what to spend. Salvaged gear gives essences of grade `sg` (set by `combine()`: the lowest grade among its inputs, Plain if none), a graded material salvages into its own grade, a crafted item never salvages into more essences than were put into it (`essIn` in `crafting.js` counts them through the recipe, so essence-then-salvage is never a gain), and a graded material's essence count follows its Plain traits; infusion overpayment is kept as `S.infCredit` (ninths); `worth()` scales by `GRADE_W`. `mkGraded` in `state.js` rebuilds graded items, so `migrate()` resets them to their fixed values. `reforge(id)` makes a new `c_` item, like old-forge tempering.
- Crafting skills: `S.skills={key:xp}` for the `SKILLS` table in `data.js` (`skillOf(item)` maps a form to a skill, `skLv(k)` gives the level from `SK_LV`, `SK_CAP` the highest result tier per level). `combine(items,{roll})` is now random per attempt (`roll` 0-100 fixes it; previews and the sim pass `roll:100`): difficulty (`CRAFT`) minus skill level gives the failure chance (`FAIL`), split into a mishap and a fizzle (`FIZ`: ingredients lost, a share of their worth refunded in embers). A result above the skill's tier cap is blocked in the forge. A clean result keeps the id `c_<hash(inputs)>` (recipe book, secrets, stacking); a mishap gets its own id and a `clean` field, and `reforge` turns it back into the clean item. The first forge never fails. Merge gains above `SOFT` count half; upgrading a crafted item of the same form adds difficulty.
- Sound: `sfx(name,delay)` in `audio.js` is a safe no-op without a user gesture or Web Audio; add a sound as one line in `SND` (built from `osc`/`noise`) and a gap in `GAP` if it needs rate limiting. The preference is in `localStorage` key `everforge-sound`, never in `S`.
- Lifetime counters live in `S.stats` (`statsDef()` in `state.js`; `migrate()` merges missing fields, deriving what it can). Count in the central places (`strike`, `dealt()` before any `B.hp-=`, enemy hit path, `enemyDown`, `youFall`, `finishDaily`, `gainEmbers`/`spendEmbers`) and show them in `renderStats()` (Codex tab).

## Testing

`node tools/smoke.js [steps]` plays the game headlessly through the real UI (Playwright + Chromium) and fails on any page error or if the run gets stuck. It plays twice: a **fresh** save played naturally (no forced unlocks, zones, Heat, daily or peddler: systems open by their own rules and the bot clicks the intro cards' Got it), and an **unlocked** save (a fresh state with every `UNLOCKS` entry open and read) that carries the feature checks gating would keep out of a 400-step newcomer run (abilities, Heat, daily, buying, selling, upgrading, reforging, fleeing, being hunted, every affix, tiles). It then replays every save in `tools/fixtures/`: each older one must load as a fully unlocked veteran with no intro waiting. When you bump the save version, run `node tools/smoke.js 400 --write-fixture` once to add a fixture for the new version (it is written from the unlocked run).

`node tools/unlock-check.js [--shots dir]` is a targeted check of the unlock framework: a fresh save is minimal (teasers, hidden counters, a map without gated POIs), each unlock fires once with its sound and intro, intros queue one at a time and Got it works, the Guide lists them, `genMap` gates POI types (reachability, counts and the seeded daily's determinism hold), and the v13 fixture migrates fully unlocked. With `--shots` it saves screenshots at 390 and 360.

`node tools/affix-check.js` is a targeted check of the affix system: every affix's HUD chip and essence drop, each hook (Vampiric, Thorned, Regenerating, Frenzied, Warded, Elusive with Sharp and Study, Splitting once, Volatile when you survive, die or guard, Swift-footed on the map and in a fight), grade rolls, roll rules, daily determinism under two `Math.random` streams, and the v12 -> v13 migration with an open elite battle.

### Balance simulator

`node tools/sim.js` runs the real combat and map code (`startBattle`, `attack`, `parry`, `ability`, `useItem`, `pstats()`, `step()`...) headlessly in Chromium, with `render`/`save`/`fx`/`sfx`/`toast` stubbed. It builds player kits by actually crafting with `combine()` (the kit table at the top of `tools/sim.js`: balanced `melee`, `defensive`, `staff` (a staff and a shield, Arcane stacked) and a min-maxed `stacked` kit at woods/pass/hollow/spire "end", plus a `starter` kit, the fresh-save sword; every recipe is checked with `checkMix` and any warning is printed) and prints each kit's `pstats()`, essence use (in plain-essence equivalents) and the embers to upgrade its graded essences. It fights every enemy at each Heat with three bot policies: `passive` (attack only), `basic` (attack, guard on a telegraphed special) and `smart` (parry telegraphed specials, use the ability when ready, heal below 35% HP). The codex is pre-mastered for the kit's zone and earlier ones (`--no-mastered` to turn that off).

The main pairing is **arrival**: the kit of the previous stage against the next zone (starter kit against the woods); **on-stage** (the kit's own zone) is a second table. Skills: each stage kit assumes the crafting-skill XP in `STAGES[..].sk` (a focused player's skills at that point); the sim builds the clean result (`roll:100`) and reports the crafts, expected attempts and expected fizzles a player would need. Per row: win%, mean turns and mean HP left% per Heat. Flags (Heat 0 unless noted): `TOO_EASY` (win 100%, over 85% HP left and under 4 turns, under 10 for a boss), `TOO_HARD` (wins under 50%) and `SLOG` (mean turns over 30, 50 for a boss, any Heat). `--attrition` adds expeditions of 4 regulars and a boss on one HP bar (35% heals after fights 2 and 4) with clear %. The economy section plays whole expeditions through `step()` on the grid (BFS to POIs, then the lair; steps, steps in the dark and torch left are reported) (enemies roam through the same `foesAct`; the `by foe` column counts fights an enemy started) and reports embers, essences and items per expedition, with and without the salvage value of the new loot (the bot never salvages), and the peddler's price range.

```
node tools/sim.js                                  # full matrix, 40 fights per cell, about 15 s
node tools/sim.js --n 100 --seed 7 --attrition --out sim.json
node tools/sim.js --kit stacked --zone spire --heat 0,5 --policy smart
node tools/sim.js --enemy wolf,imp --elite          # --elite adds a magic (+) and a rare (*) variant of each regular enemy
node tools/sim.js --rarity --seed 7 --n 100         # the rarity table (see Affixes), no matrix and no economy
node tools/sim.js --fresh --exped 6 --seed 3        # the first expeditions of a new save with the unlock gates on
```

`--rarity` prints the **rarity table**: the arrival pairing (each kit against the next zone's regular enemies at map row `--row`), every enemy, with every affix in turn (the first affix cycles through all that species can roll; a rare's others are rolled), win%, mean turns and HP left for normal, magic and rare at Heat 0, 3 and 5 for each policy, plus a win% per first affix. It flags `RARE_HARD` (smart play winning under 85% at Heat 0-3) and `SLOG`. The target: a rare is a real fight but smart play with a stage kit still wins over 85% at Heat 0-3. `tools/smoke.js` also runs every affix once through the real UI, three rare combinations, and fails on a stuck fight or a missing essence.

`--fresh` plays the first `--exped` expeditions of a new save (starter kit, first `--policy`) with the unlock gates on, and prints per expedition the POIs on the map, what the bot met, and what opened afterwards; every other mode opens all unlocks first, so its numbers do not depend on the unlock order.

Flags: `--n` fights per cell, `--zone`/`--enemy`/`--kit`/`--heat`/`--policy` (comma lists; `--kit` matches names like `melee`, `spire`, `staff/pass`; `--zone` keeps enemies of those zones and the kits whose own or arrival zone is listed), `--seed` (seeds `Math.random` per cell, so results are reproducible), `--row` (map row 0-5 used to scale regular enemies, default 3), `--all` (also print the other pairings), `--attrition`, `--no-mastered`, `--jobs` (browser pages in parallel), `--exped` (expeditions per zone), `--trace` (print one battle log per selected cell instead of the matrix, e.g. `--trace --kit defensive/spire --enemy archon --heat 5 --policy smart`), `--no-econ` / `--econ-only`, `--out` (JSON path). Change the numbers in `data.js`, rerun with the same `--seed` and compare.
