## Candidate decision

Raise `GEN_N` to 12 for the four generated authored zones. Keep `RIFT_N=10`: the task asks for additional authored roster variety, while Rift already samples biome identities and its master profile is accepted separately. Scale each non-Rift generated foe's encounter weight to `10/GEN_N`, so a larger roster does not increase the total generated encounter budget. Preserve the alpha's legacy slot with the original ten-slot selector; the two new entries remain ordinary wilds. Fen remains authored-only. This adds two deterministic generated creatures per applicable zone without changing map fight/elite counts, Fen's roster, the Rift's roster, or wild-affix drop suppression.
